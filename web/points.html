Signal construction (fixed ex ante)

Four cross-asset factors. Each is standardised to a z-score on an expanding window (mean and standard deviation computed from all history up to that session; no look-ahead). The composite is the equal-weighted average of the four. A signal is triggered when the composite exceeds its 90th percentile on that same expanding window. Two conditional triggers are added: within 3 calendar days of a CPI release, or 3-7 days of an NFP release, with the composite at or above its expanding median. No signal on the FOMC decision day.
